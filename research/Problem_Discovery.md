# Problem Discovery & Product Hypothesis

**Core problem:** In HCMC, a rider planning a journey spanning ride-hailing and public transport has to reconcile multiple timetables, access points, transfers, fare estimates and first-/last-mile movement. For students and price-sensitive commuters, transit can be attractive but coordination adds friction.

**Proposed user:** Students and urban commuters traveling across the Metro Line 1 corridor and connecting bus networks.

**Product hypothesis:** Presenting a comparable set of Grab-only, Grab-to-bus and Grab-to-metro itineraries with estimated cost, time and transfers can improve decision confidence and enable easier multi-mode journeys.

**Scope:** Coursework prototype using route/traffic samples from HCMC around 2025; not a live, validated transit-service dataset.
